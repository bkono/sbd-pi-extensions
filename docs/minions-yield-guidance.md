# Event-driven minion supervision

Approved 2026-09-22. Tracking: `sbdpi-u0e`. Branch: `fix/minions-yield-guidance`.

## Decision ledger

- **Decision:** teach fresh-context supervisors at tool selection, dispatch, inspection, and lifecycle-result boundaries. Yielding ends the current assistant turn, preserves tokens/context, leaves children running, and keeps the supervisor available to the user.
- **Decision:** use “evaluate and decide” in the short loop; teach fix/file/reject beneath it. The supervisor owns correctness, scope, project alignment, and acceptance, not mechanical agreement with reviewers.
- **Invariant:** retain non-preemptive `followUp` delivery and serial supervisor handling. Ending a turn is neither goal completion nor process shutdown.
- **Invariant:** retain live-scope ownership, review requirements, inspection availability, and Beadwork manager-only behavior. Inspection does not acknowledge pending lifecycle packets.
- **Closed doors / Do not:** no steering, heartbeat nudges, new scheduler/wait tool, enforced polling limits, automatic result acceptance, or packet acknowledgment redesign.
- **Superseded:** “use spawn when you intend to wait” and the permissive post-dispatch menu. Replace them rather than layering contradictory guidance on top.
